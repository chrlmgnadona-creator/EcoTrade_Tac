LGU Tacloban CENRO Guidelines for Reusable Materials

  • All items listed should be clean, non-hazardous, and fit for structural, creative, or practical reuse.
  • Hazardous materials (e.g., liquid chemicals, asbestos, unsealed battery acid) are strictly prohibited.
  • Physical pickup and exchanges must take place safely at designated Barangay Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs) or public places.
